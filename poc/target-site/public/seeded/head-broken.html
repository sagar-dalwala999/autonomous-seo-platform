Head closed early by an invalid element

This page seeds the head-boundary defect. The canonical, meta robots, hreflang and Open Graph tags below the div are present in source but sit outside the effective head. Google stops reading at the first invalid element, so the canonical and hreflang are never seen. The meta robots tag is the exception: Google explicitly honours robots directives in the body, so a correct analyser must not report one blanket verdict for all of them.

What a correct crawler reports

headBoundary.closedBy should be div, closedAtOffset should point at that element, and stranded[] should carry canonical with honoured false alongside meta-robots with honoured true.
